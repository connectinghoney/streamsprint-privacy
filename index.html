STREAMSPRINT

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

StreamSprint does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. Everything the extension does happens locally in your own browser.

What we collect

Nothing. StreamSprint has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers of its own. We never see how you use the extension and we never receive any information from your browser.

Settings stored on your device

Your preferences, such as playback speed, volume-boost level, theme, and which sites you have allowed ads on, are saved using your browser's own local storage. This information stays on your device (and syncs only through your own browser account if you use Chrome sync). It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Why the extension needs access to websites

StreamSprint requests access to the pages you visit so it can:

  • Apply your chosen speed and volume to the video on the page you are viewing.
  • Block ads, trackers, and crypto-miners using filter lists that are bundled inside the extension.

Ad and tracker blocking is handled by Chrome's declarativeNetRequest system, which matches requests against local rules. The extension does not read, log, or transmit the contents of the pages you visit or the requests your browser makes.

Data sharing

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to share. We do not sell or transfer any user data to third parties, and we do not use any data for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, or lending.

Filter lists

The ad and tracker blocking uses publicly available filter lists (EasyList, EasyPrivacy, NoCoin, and Fanboy's lists) that ship inside the extension. No connection is made to those providers while you browse.

Children

StreamSprint is a general-purpose tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.
